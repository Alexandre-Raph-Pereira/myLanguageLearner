# Teaching Korean

Guidance only: always answer with the fields requested by the app.

## Speech levels
- Write examples in **해요체** (polite informal) by default: it is what learners use most
  and what TOPIK listening / reading uses in everyday dialogues.
- Show **합쇼체** (-습니다/-ㅂ니다) for announcements, news, formal writing, and when the
  grammar point itself is formal. Show **반말** (-아/어, -자, -니?) only for close friends,
  diaries and quotes, and say so in the explanation.
- TOPIK writing (쓰기) uses the plain written style (-다/-ㄴ다/-는다): mention it when a
  grammar point is typical of written Korean.
- Honorifics: mark -(으)시- and honorific verbs (드시다, 계시다, 주무시다) when the subject is
  a person to respect; don't mix honorific and plain forms in one example.

## Particles
- Explain a particle by its function and by contrast: 은/는 (topic, contrast) vs 이/가
  (new information, subject focus), 을/를 (object), 에 (place of being / time /
  destination) vs 에서 (place of action / origin), 도, 만, (으)로, 와/과 · 하고 · (이)랑.
- Always show the consonant / vowel alternation (받침): 책**은**, 사과**는**.
- In casual examples particles may be dropped; in teaching examples keep them.

## Conjugation and irregulars
- Describe formation from the stem: vowel 아 vs 어 (ㅏ/ㅗ → 아, else 어, 하다 → 해),
  and whether the ending attaches differently after a final consonant (-(으)면, -(으)니까).
- Name the irregular when it applies and give one example form each time:
  - ㅂ: 덥다 → 더워요 (but 입다 → 입어요 is regular)
  - ㄷ: 듣다 → 들어요 (but 받다 → 받아요 is regular)
  - ㅅ: 짓다 → 지어요 (but 웃다 → 웃어요 is regular)
  - 르: 모르다 → 몰라요, 부르다 → 불러요
  - ㅎ: 그렇다 → 그래요, 하얗다 → 하얘요
  - ㄹ: 살다 → 사세요, 삽니다, 사는 (ㄹ drops before ㄴ, ㅂ, ㅅ)
  - also 으 drop (쓰다 → 써요) and 하다 → 해요.

## Spacing (띄어쓰기)
Write examples with standard spacing: particles attach to the word (학교에); dependent
nouns are spaced (먹을 수 있다, 할 것이다, 간 적이 있다); auxiliary verbs are spaced in
teaching examples (먹어 보다, 해 주다); numbers + counters as usual (사과 두 개, 3시).

## Romanization
No romanization unless the learner's request asks for it. Hangul only.

## Typical mistakes of English / French speakers
- Overusing 저는/나는 where Korean drops the subject; overusing 그/그녀.
- 은/는 vs 이/가; 에 vs 에서; forgetting the object particle with 하다 nouns vs 하다 verbs.
- Literal word order from English/French; adjectives used as verbs are not followed by
  이다 (예뻐요, not 예쁘이에요).
- Tense with -아/어서 (never past tense before -아/어서), -(으)니까 vs -아/어서 with commands.
- French speakers: tu/vous mapped to 반말/존댓말 too directly — explain the social cues.

## Text
Korean in Unicode NFC: composed syllables (한), never decomposed jamo sequences.
