## Description

`-아/어 버리다` says that an action is **completely finished**, and adds the speaker's **feeling** about it. The same form carries two opposite feelings; context tells which one.

1. **Relief — "got it done, finally"**: the task is over and its burden is gone. 숙제를 다 끝내 **버렸어요** = I finished all my homework (and what a relief).
2. **Regret / sadness — "ended up…, it's gone"**: something irreversible happened, usually against the speaker's wish. 친구가 고향으로 돌아가 **버렸어요** = my friend went back home (and I miss them).
3. **Decisive / impulsive action**: doing something in one go, sometimes on a whim. 화가 나서 다 말해 **버렸어요** = I got angry and just told everything.

## Formation

- Stem with **ㅏ / ㅗ** + `아 버리다`: 가다 → 가 **버리다**, 오다 → 와 **버리다**, 팔다 → 팔**아 버리다**.
- Other stems + `어 버리다`: 먹다 → 먹**어 버리다**, 마시다 → 마셔 **버리다**, 끝내다 → 끝내 **버리다**.
- 하다 → 해 **버리다**: 결정하다 → 결정해 **버리다**.
- Irregulars follow the usual `-아/어` rules: 듣다 → 들**어 버리다**, 모르다 → 몰라 **버리다**, 쓰다 → 써 **버리다**.
- `버리다` is conjugated normally, mostly in the past: `-아/어 버렸어요`, `-아/어 버렸습니다`. Also as a decision or command: `-아/어 버릴 거예요` (I'm just going to…), `-아/어 버려!` (just do it / get rid of it!).
- Verbs only (no adjectives).
- Common fixed forms: 잊어버리다 (to forget completely), 잃어버리다 (to lose) are written as one word.

## Examples

| Use      | Korean                                                     | English                                                        |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Relief   | 숙제를 모두 끝내 **버렸습니다**.                           | I've finished all my homework (what a relief).                 |
| Relief   | 밀린 빨래를 다 해 **버렸어요**.                            | I got all the piled-up laundry done.                           |
| Regret   | 기숙사에서 함께 살던 친구가 고향으로 돌아가 **버렸습니다**. | The friend I lived with in the dorm went back home (sadly).    |
| Regret   | 버스가 벌써 떠나 **버렸어요**.                             | The bus has already left.                                      |
| Regret   | 동생이 내 케이크를 먹**어 버렸어요**.                      | My little brother ate my cake (all of it).                     |
| Decisive | 화가 나서 그 사람한테 다 말해 **버렸어요**.                | I got angry and just told him everything.                      |

## Similar · be careful

- **vs `-고 말다`**: also "ended up…" with regret, but stresses an outcome that happened despite effort or wishes and sounds more written. `-아/어 버리다` is more conversational and can also express relief.
- **vs plain past**: 친구가 돌아갔어요 is a neutral fact; 친구가 돌아가 **버렸어요** adds the feeling (sadness, "gone for good").
- **One feeling or the other**: the form itself does not say relief or regret — read the context (finishing a chore → relief, losing something → regret).
- **잊어버리다 / 잃어버리다** are lexicalised: they are the normal words for "forget" and "lose", not always emotional.

## Related
