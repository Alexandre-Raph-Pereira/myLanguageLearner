## Description

`-도록` links an action to the result it aims at or reaches. Three uses:

1. **Purpose — "so that…"**: the first clause is the goal, the second is what you do to reach it. Very often negative (`-지 않도록` = "so that … not").
2. **`-도록 하다` — "make sure to…"**: a polite instruction or recommendation. With `-겠다` it becomes a commitment ("I'll make sure to…"). It insists on the result but stays polite and slightly formal (doctors, teachers, announcements), softer than a bare `-(으)세요`.
3. **Extent — "until / to the point that…"**: shows the intensity or duration of an action, often with a physical result.

## Formation

- Verb **or adjective** stem + `도록`, the same after a vowel or a consonant: 가다 → 가**도록**, 먹다 → 먹**도록**, 아프다 → 아프**도록**.
- Negative: stem + `-지 않도록` / `-지 못하도록`.
- No past tense before it (~~갔도록~~); the tense goes on the main verb.
- Use 2: stem + `도록 하다`, conjugated normally: `-도록 하세요`, `-도록 해요`, `-도록 하겠습니다`.

## Examples

| Use          | Korean                                           | English                                        |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Purpose      | 잊지 않**도록** 메모했어요.                      | I took notes so that I wouldn't forget.        |
| Purpose      | 감기에 걸리지 않**도록** 옷을 따뜻하게 입으세요. | Dress warmly so you don't catch a cold.        |
| Purpose      | 아이가 잘 수 있**도록** 조용히 해 주세요.        | Please be quiet so the child can sleep.        |
| Make sure to | 일찍 자**도록 하세요**.                          | Make sure to go to bed early.                  |
| Make sure to | 내일까지 보고서를 보내**도록 하겠습니다**.       | I'll make sure to send the report by tomorrow. |
| Extent       | 목이 아프**도록** 노래했어요.                    | I sang until my throat hurt.                   |
| Extent       | 밤새**도록** 이야기했어요.                       | We talked all night long.                      |

## Similar · be careful

- **vs `-기 위해(서)`**: also purpose, but same subject in both clauses and verbs only. `-도록` allows different subjects (아이가 잘 수 있**도록** _(제가)_ 조용히 해요) and adjectives or results you don't fully control (잊지 않도록).
- **vs `-(으)려고`**: intention, same subject, and **cannot** be followed by an order or suggestion (~~늦지 않으려고 일찍 오세요~~). `-도록` can (늦지 않**도록** 일찍 오세요).
- **vs `-게`**: in casual speech `-게` often replaces `-도록` for purpose (늦지 않**게** 오세요).
- **vs `-(으)ㄹ 때까지`**: "until" a point in time. The "until" of `-도록` is about intensity and is often idiomatic (밤새도록, 배가 터지도록).
- **`-도록 하다` can also mean "make / let someone do"** (선생님이 학생들에게 책을 읽**도록 했어요**), close to `-게 하다`. Context tells it apart from "make sure to".
