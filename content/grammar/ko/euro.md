## Description

`-(으)로` is a noun particle that shows **the way** something happens. Its meaning comes from the noun and the verb around it. Ask yourself one question:

1. **Direction — "towards / to"**: *where to?* 고향**으로** 가요 = I go (towards) my hometown. 오른쪽**으로** 가세요 = go right.
2. **Means / tool / method — "by / with / in"**: *using what?* 펜**으로** 써요 = I write with a pen. 버스**로** 가요 = I go by bus. 한국어**로** 말해요 = I speak in Korean.
3. **Role / capacity — "as"**: *in what role?* 선생님**으로** 일해요 = I work as a teacher. 선물**로** 줬어요 = I gave it as a present.

Also common: material (나무**로** 만든 의자 = a chair made of wood), cause (감기**로** 결석했어요 = absent because of a cold), change into (`-(으)로 바뀌다 / 바꾸다`) and choice when ordering (커피**로** 주세요 = I'll have coffee).

## Formation

- Noun ending in a vowel **or ㄹ** + `로`: 버스 → 버스**로**, 학교 → 학교**로**, 서울 → 서울**로**, 연필 → 연필**로**, 지하철 → 지하철**로**.
- Noun ending in another consonant + `으로`: 펜 → 펜**으로**, 집 → 집**으로**, 손 → 손**으로**, 한국 → 한국**으로**.
- Formal / written variants: `-(으)로서` stresses "**as** (a role)", `-(으)로써` stresses "**by means of**".
- Direction words: 이쪽**으로**, 앞**으로**, 위**로**, 밖**으로**.

| Tool       | 펜**으로** 써요.               | I write with a pen.            |
| Tool       | 칼**로** 잘라요.               | I cut it with a knife.         |
| Transport  | 버스**로** 가요.               | I go by bus.                   |

## Similar · be careful

- **vs `에` (direction)**: both work with movement verbs, but `에` marks the **destination** (arriving there: 학교**에** 가요) while `-(으)로` marks the **direction / route** (heading that way: 학교 쪽**으로** 가요). With 쪽, 오른쪽, 앞, 위… `-(으)로` is the natural choice.
- **`에` is never "with / by"**: 펜**으로** 써요 = I write with a pen; 펜**에** 써요 would mean "I write *on* the pen".
- **ㄹ-final nouns take `로`, not `으로`**: ~~서울으로~~ → 서울**로**, ~~연필으로~~ → 연필**로**.
- **Transport**: 버스**로** 가요 and 버스를 타고 가요 both mean "I go by bus"; the second is more frequent in speech.
- **vs `-(으)로서` / `-(으)로써`**: written forms that make the role (서) or the means (써) explicit; in speech plain `-(으)로` covers both.
