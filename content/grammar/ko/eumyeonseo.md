---
title: -(으)면서
pattern: -(으)면서
variants: -면서, -으면서, -이면서, -면서도, -으면서도, -이면서도
meaning: while (doing two things at once); both … and; even though
level: 2
---

## Description

`-(으)면서` links two actions or states that happen **at the same time**, done by the **same subject**.

1. **Simultaneous actions — "while…"**: two things done at once (multitasking). 음악을 들으**면서** 공부해요 = I study while listening to music.
2. **Two states at once — "both … and / … yet …"**: with adjectives or nouns, two qualities held together, often contrasting. 이 빵은 맛있으**면서** 싸요 = this bread is tasty and (yet) cheap.
3. **`-(으)면서도` — "even though…"**: adding `도` stresses the contrast between the two clauses. 알**면서도** 모르는 척해요 = he pretends not to know even though he knows.

## Formation

- Stem ending in a vowel or **ㄹ** + `면서`: 보다 → 보**면서**, 마시다 → 마시**면서**, 살다 → 살**면서**, 만들다 → 만들**면서**.
- Stem ending in another consonant + `으면서`: 먹다 → 먹**으면서**, 읽다 → 읽**으면서**, 맛있다 → 맛있**으면서**.
- Irregulars: 듣다 → 들**으면서**, 걷다 → 걸**으면서**, 덥다 → 더우**면서**.
- Noun: `N(이)면서` — `이면서` after a consonant, `면서` (or `이면서`) after a vowel: 학생**이면서** 선생님, 가수**면서** 배우.
- No past tense before it (~~먹었으면서~~ for "while"); the tense goes on the final verb: 밥을 먹**으면서** TV를 **봤어요**.
- Both clauses have the **same subject**.

## Examples

| Use                | Korean                                     | English                                                  |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| While              | 민수는 텔레비전을 보**면서** 밥을 먹어요.  | Minsu eats while watching TV.                            |
| While              | 음악을 들**으면서** 공부해요.              | I study while listening to music.                        |
| While              | 운전하**면서** 전화하면 안 돼요.           | You mustn't make calls while driving.                    |
| Two states         | 이 빵은 맛있**으면서** 싸요.               | This bread is tasty and cheap.                           |
| Noun               | 그 사람은 가수**이면서** 배우예요.         | That person is both a singer and an actor.               |
| Even though (-도)  | 알**면서도** 모르는 척했어요.              | He pretended not to know even though he knew.            |

## Similar · be careful

- **vs `-고`**: `-고` just lists actions or states ("and", often "and then"); it says nothing about simultaneity. 밥을 먹**고** TV를 봐요 = I eat and then watch TV. 밥을 먹**으면서** TV를 봐요 = I eat while watching TV.
- **vs `-는 동안`**: also "while", but the subjects can differ and it focuses on the time span (제가 요리하**는 동안** 동생이 청소했어요). `-(으)면서` needs one subject for both clauses.
- **vs `-(으)며`**: written / formal equivalent of `-(으)면서`; also used to list (`-고`).
- **With a noun, don't forget `(이)`**: ~~학생면서~~ → 학생**이면서**.
