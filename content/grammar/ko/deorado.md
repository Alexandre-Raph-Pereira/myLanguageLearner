## Description

`-더라도` = "even if", with the stress on **supposing**: the speaker imagines a case (often unlikely, difficult or extreme) and says that the second clause stays true anyway. It sounds firmer and slightly more formal than `-아/어도`.

1. **Hypothetical "even if"**: 무슨 일이 있더라도 = whatever happens.
2. **Accepting a difficulty, then advice or resolution**: 힘들더라도 조금만 참으세요 = even if it's hard, hang in there a little. The second clause is very often an order, advice, promise or obligation (`-(으)세요`, `-(으)ㄹ게요`, `-아/어야 하다`).

Often reinforced with **아무리** (no matter how), **설령 / 비록** (even supposing).

## Formation

- Verb or adjective stem + `더라도`, the same after a vowel or a consonant: 가다 → 가**더라도**, 먹다 → 먹**더라도**, 바쁘다 → 바쁘**더라도**.
- Past: `-았/었더라도` (실패했더라도 = even if I had failed).
- Noun: `N(이)더라도`: 학생**이더라도**, 친구**더라도**.
- Not for a fact that already happened and is known: for "even though it rained yesterday" use 비가 왔**어도** / 왔지만, not ~~비가 오더라도~~.

## Examples

| Use          | Korean                                      | English                                                        |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hypothetical | 무슨 일이 있**더라도** 약속은 지킬게요.     | Whatever happens, I'll keep my promise.                        |
| Hypothetical | 비가 오**더라도** 경기는 진행됩니다.        | Even if it rains, the match will go ahead.                     |
| Hypothetical | 설령 실패하**더라도** 후회하지 않을 거예요. | Even if I fail, I won't regret it.                             |
| Advice       | 힘들**더라도** 포기하지 마세요.             | Even if it's hard, don't give up.                              |
| Advice       | 아무리 바쁘**더라도** 건강을 챙겨야 해요.   | No matter how busy you are, you must look after your health.   |
| Past         | 그때 알았**더라도** 결과는 같았을 거예요.   | Even if I had known then, the result would have been the same. |
| Noun         | 친한 친구**더라도** 비밀은 지켜야 해요.     | Even if it's a close friend, you have to keep the secret.      |

## Similar · be careful

- **vs `-아/어도`**: same core meaning and often interchangeable in hypotheses (비가 와도 / 비가 오더라도). Differences: `-더라도` insists on the supposition and sounds stronger and more formal; `-아/어도` is more casual and also works for real, known facts (약을 먹어도 안 나아요 — not ~~먹더라도~~ for a fact).
- **Don't confuse with `-더라` / `-더라고요`** (reporting what you saw or experienced): 맛있**더라고요** = it was tasty (I found).
- **vs `-(이)라도`**: `-(이)라도` is a noun particle (커피라도 = at least coffee); `N이더라도` is "even if it is N".

## Related
