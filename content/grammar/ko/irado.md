title: -(이)라도
pattern: -(이)라도
variants: -이라도, -라도
meaning: at least / if nothing else; even; (question word +) any-
level: 3

## Description

`-(이)라도` marks something as **not ideal, but acceptable**. Three uses:

1. **"At least…" / "if nothing else…"**: the most common use. The speaker settles for, suggests or offers a lesser option because the ideal one isn't possible ("better than nothing"). Very common in suggestions (`-(으)ㄹ까요?`, `-(으)세요`) and offers (`-(으)ㄹ게요`).
2. **"Even…"**: the least expected or most extreme case is still included (어린아이라도 알아요 = even a child knows that).
3. **Question word + `(이)라도` = "any-"**: 누구라도 (anyone), 언제라도 (any time), 어디라도 (anywhere), 무엇이라도 / 뭐라도 (anything).

## Formation

- Noun ending in a **consonant** + `이라도`: 물 → 물**이라도**, 빵 → 빵**이라도**.
- Noun ending in a **vowel** + `라도`: 커피 → 커피**라도**, 저 → 저**라도**.
- Replaces the particles 이/가 and 을/를 (~~물을이라도~~), but goes **after** other particles: 친구에게**라도**, 집에서**라도**.
- Also after some adverbs and time words: 조금**이라도** (even a little), 지금**이라도** (even now, it's not too late), 한 번**이라도** (even once).

## Examples

| Use      | Korean                                    | English                                                 |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| At least | 시간이 없으면 커피**라도** 마셔요.        | If you don't have time, at least have a coffee.         |
| At least | 물**이라도** 드세요.                      | At least have some water.                               |
| At least | 전화**라도** 해 주세요.                   | At least give me a call.                                |
| At least | 비가 오니까 영화**라도** 볼까요?          | It's raining, so shall we at least watch a movie?       |
| At least | 커피는 없지만 차**라도** 드릴게요.        | We don't have coffee, but I can at least offer you tea. |
| At least | 다른 사람이 없으면 저**라도** 갈게요.     | If no one else can, I'll go.                            |
| Even     | 그건 어린아이**라도** 알아요.             | Even a child knows that.                                |
| Even     | 지금**이라도** 시작하세요.                | Start now; it's not too late.                           |
| Any-     | 궁금한 게 있으면 언제**라도** 연락하세요. | If you have questions, contact me any time.             |

## Similar · be careful

- **vs `-만` ("only")**: 물**만** 마셨어요 = I drank only water (a plain fact). 물**이라도** 마셨어요 = I at least drank some water (a lesser option, accepted with some relief).
- **vs `-도` ("also / even")**: 저**도** 갈게요 = I'll go too (inclusion). 저**라도** 갈게요 = if no one else can go, I'll go (stepping in as a fallback).
- **vs `-(이)나` ("… or something")**: 영화**나** 볼까요? is casual and indifferent ("a movie or whatever"). 영화**라도** 볼까요? adds "since we can't do better". Often interchangeable in suggestions.
- **vs `-아/어도` ("even if")**: `-(이)라도` attaches to **nouns**; for verbs and adjectives use `-아/어도` (비가 와**도** 가요). With nouns, `N이어도 / N여도` is close to use 2.
- **vs `-더라도` ("even if", stronger supposition)**: also a verb ending, see its lesson: 힘들**더라도** 포기하지 마세요.

## Related

- contrast: -만 (only, no "better than nothing" nuance)
- contrast: -도 (also, inclusion without fallback)
- similar: -(이)나 (… or something, casual suggestion)
- contrast: -아/어도 (even if, connecting ending on verbs and adjectives)
- contrast: -더라도 (even if, stronger hypothetical, on verbs and adjectives)
